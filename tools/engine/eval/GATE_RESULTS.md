# Gate evaluation: grounding gate and injection gate

Generated by `python tools/engine/eval/gate_eval.py --write` from the JSON files in `tools/engine/eval/data/`. Offline and deterministic. Corpus sources, licences, construction and limits are in `docs/EVAL.md`; read that before quoting a number.

Positive class is the bad item (unsupported claim, injection attempt). False-accept rate is the share of bad items the gate lets through; false-reject rate is the share of good items it stops. Brackets are 95% Wilson intervals, then (k/n).

Precision depends on the ratio of bad to good items in the corpus, which was chosen by hand, so it is not a field estimate. The two rates are less sensitive to that mix.
